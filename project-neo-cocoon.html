Neo Cocoon: Portable Neonatal Incubator

The Neo Cocoon is a portable, low-cost neonatal incubator designed to provide a stable thermal environment for newborns in resource-limited settings. My team and I developed this project at BMS College of Engineering to address the high cost and immobility of traditional incubators.

Problem

Commercial neonatal incubators are expensive and bulky, making them inaccessible in many rural hospitals and clinics.

Solution

We designed a portable, cost-effective incubator using off-the-shelf components, reducing the overall cost by 60%.

My Role

  • Lead hardware and PCB designer
  • Embedded systems programmer
  • Validation and testing lead

Technical Stack & Features

  • **Embedded Systems:** Used an Arduino microcontroller for temperature and humidity control.
  • **Sensor Integration:** Integrated a multi-sensor array for real-time monitoring of temperature, humidity, and SpO₂, achieving 95% accuracy.
  • **Hardware Design:** Designed a power-efficient PCB layout using KiCad and fabricated the prototype.
  • **Compliance:** Performed validation testing in accordance with IEC 60601 standards for medical electrical equipment.

Outcome & Impact

The Neo Cocoon successfully maintained a stable environment with high accuracy. The project was recognized for its potential to democratize neonatal care, and we co-authored technical documentation and user manuals for future deployment. This project showcases my ability to transition a complex biomedical problem into a functional, validated product.
